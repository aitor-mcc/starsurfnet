About this Webzine

So, one day I wondered, are there any cool magazines to suit my tastes in music, art, fashion, culture, etc...? and I found I wasn't really able to find any good ones to suit my tastes, perhaps since I always (annoyingly) seem to choose the more niche styles within most media to love.

After being a little annoyed for a bit I thought to myself, hey wouldn't this be a good opportunity to expand my personal website by creating a space where I can write and research about things that interest me? Further, this is a great chance for me to explore the cultures, movements, aesthetics and countless other things that might interest me but I previously had no reason to really explore. It just seems like a perfect project to fulfill all these things I've been interested in.

Be warned, however, since this is just a little fun thing for me, please expect irregular posts on random topics and while I'll try my best to be as accurate as possible, I'm by no means qualified to talk about anything at all, so you should be critical yourself of anything I write...

Policies

  • I will make sure to not use any AI for any of the content of this website and I will avoid talking about AI generated content.

  • All photos are stolen, none are mine unless stated otherwise. All written content in the articles will be completely original and any quotes will be clearly stated.

  • I do not pretend to know anything about anything and I will try to be as accurate as possible. If you have a problem with what I have written please do contact me!

  • All sources used will be referenced at the bottom of the articles, please read them if you find the article interesting!
